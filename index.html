FAQ

A Few Things Worth Knowing

What is Mogoi-AI exactly? ▼
Mogoi-AI is an always-on autonomous AI daemon with desktop awareness, multi-agent hierarchy, visual workflows, and goal pursuit. It works as a co-founder that never sleeps — programming the repeatable parts and using AI for the parts that need thought.
Can I self-host instead? ▼
Yes. Mogoi-AI is fully source-available and free to self-host. Run it on your own hardware with your own model keys. Docker, binary, and WSL2 support all included.
Does a single prompt give me a finished workflow? ▼
Not exactly — and that's by design. Mogoi-AI helps you spot what to automate, builds the steps with you, and uses AI where judgment matters. You review every workflow before it publishes.
How does awareness work? ▼
Awareness observes your work patterns and surfaces signals worth discussing. Repeated app switching, repeated tasks, or forgotten goals — Mogoi-AI flags them so you can decide whether to automate.
How much control do I have? ▼
Complete control. The Authority engine gates every action at runtime. All tool executions are logged in an audit trail. Sensitive operations require explicit approval via dashboard, voice, or Telegram.
